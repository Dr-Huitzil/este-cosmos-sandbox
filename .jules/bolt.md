## 2025-02-28 - Performance Optimization: O(N^2 log N) degradation from redundant utility function sorting
**Learning:** Found a critical performance anti-pattern where utility functions (`calculateMPG`) were performing internal `.sort()` operations (`O(N log N)`) on collections inside of mapping loops or render cycles, leading to `O(N^2 log N)` or redundant sorting behaviors. The codebase architecture relies on `FleetContext` hooks (like `useFuel`) to provide pre-sorted arrays (e.g., `sortedFuelEntries` sorted newest-first) specifically to prevent this.
**Action:** When updating or refactoring utility functions that process collections, ensure they accept pre-sorted arrays as parameters instead of internally sorting them. Always trace back to `FleetContext` or the component's internal `useMemo` hooks to find the pre-sorted collections and pass those directly to utility functions.

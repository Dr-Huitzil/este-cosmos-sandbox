## 2024-03-21 - [O(N² log N) degradation due to nested utility sorting]
**Learning:** Utilities that aggregate collection data (e.g., `calculateMPG`) should *never* handle their own internal sorting if they are called inside render loops mapping over collections. In this codebase, the original `calculateMPG` utility sorted the raw `fuelEntries` internally. Since it was called during the map operation inside `FuelLogTable` and `AnalyticsContext`, it caused a redundant `O(N log N)` sort operation *for every single row*, leading to a degrading `O(N² log N)` performance bottleneck.
**Action:** Utility functions calculating metrics over collections must expect and rely on the pre-sorted arrays (`sortedFuelEntries`, `sortedServiceEntries`, etc.) that are natively provided by the `FleetContext` hooks. This ensures the expensive `O(N log N)` sorting happens strictly once per dependency change via `useMemo` at the source.

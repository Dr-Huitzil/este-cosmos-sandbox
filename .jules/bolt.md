## 2024-05-24 - Context pre-sorting for map optimization
**Learning:** The application heavily relies on React Context (`FleetContext`) to distribute pre-sorted arrays (like `sortedFuelEntries` sorting newest-first). However, some utility functions like `calculateMPG` were performing their own `[...array].sort()` operations internally. When these utilities are called inside `.map()` loops during rendering (e.g., in `fuelLogTable` or `AnalyticsContext`), it causes a severe $O(N^2 \log N)$ performance degradation.
**Action:** Always inspect utility functions that iterate over collections to ensure they don't perform redundant sorts. They should accept and strictly document that they require pre-sorted arrays provided by the Context architecture. Update callers to use stable, pre-sorted arrays (like `sortedFuelEntriesRef.current` or `sortedEntries` from `useMemo`) rather than raw `entries`.
